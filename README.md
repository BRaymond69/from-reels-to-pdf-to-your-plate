# 🍳 Reel → Recipe Card 📜

**Reel → Recipe Card** is a full-stack web application designed to instantly turn social media cooking videos into clean, printable kitchen guides!

By simply pasting an Instagram Reel or TikTok video URL, the app extracts the video's transcript, caption, and thumbnail. It then uses a *free-tier LLM* to parse that raw information into a beautifully formatted, single-page printable PDF recipe card—complete with localized ingredient measurements and step-by-step instructions.

---

### 💡 Why I Created This

* **Eliminate the Clutter:** Social media captions are usually messy, filled with *hashtags*, *emojis*, and *rambling stories*. This tool isolates **just the ingredients and directions** you actually need.
* **Hands-Free Cooking:** Swiping through a dynamic video with *sticky, flour-covered fingers* is a nightmare. A static, single-page PDF card (or printed paper) is much easier to read while active in the kitchen.
* **Organized Recipe Library:** Instead of losing saved posts in endless bookmark collections, this project automatically categorizes dishes into a *structured digital cookbook* mapped by region, cuisine, and dietary preferences.
* **Multi-Language Normalization:** Translating global dishes can be tricky—this tool automatically converts raw recipe text into your preferred language (*English, French, or Korean*) while adjusting unit measurements so they make sense for local cooks! 🧑‍🍳✨
